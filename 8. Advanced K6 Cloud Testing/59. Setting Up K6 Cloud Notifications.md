It's possible to setup notifications about your test. So that, you and your can get notifications about when the test started running, when did it stopped, if it got executed successfully, and more. To create notifications, follow the below steps:
.
	- In Grafana Cloud, navigate to **Testing & Synthetics** -> **Settings** -> **Notifications** -> **Notifications**.
	- Select the appropriate method as per your requirements and follow the necessary steps to setup.
